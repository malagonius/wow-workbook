# Lichborn — Brainstorming Notes

This file intentionally contains ideas that are useful to remember but are **not necessarily decisions**.

## Fantasy phrases

> My death presence will eventually succumb you.

> Kill it before it kills you, because it will.

> You're not fighting me. You're fighting the army I've already taken from you.

> You cannot escape what your death has already become.

## Dominion progression

A possible emotional progression for the target:

- **Low:** Something is wrong.
- **Medium:** This thing is killing me.
- **High:** I am losing control.
- **Extreme:** I belong to the Lich.

This is a fantasy guide, not a confirmed UI/mechanical scale.

## Potential design tension

There may be an interesting choice between:

- **Consume Dominion now** for immediate power.
- **Keep Dominion** to reach stronger control or a stronger death consequence.

This could make the Lich feel methodical without making every fight identical.

## Pet consequence idea

A target's eventual servant could reflect **how** the Lich dominated it rather than simply being a generic undead summon.

For example, a damage-oriented expression could produce a damage-oriented servant, while a control-oriented expression could produce a control-oriented servant.

This is intentionally loose until the core Dominion mechanic is defined.

## Existing-spec differentiation notes

Avoid:

- Blood-style survivability as the main identity.
- Frost-style burst/destructive cold as the main identity.
- Unholy-style disease + undead army as the main identity.
- Deathbringer-style generic death/inevitability fantasy without a distinct gameplay hook.

The new spec should make the player feel that **the enemy is progressively becoming the Lich's property**.
