# Lichborn Spells

The current spellbook for the Lichborn (4th Death Knight specialization).

Numbers and some names are intentionally provisional while the core design is being developed.

## Rune Spells

| Spell | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Dominate** | Melee | 1 Rune | Minor damage. Applies Death's Claim. |
| **Death's Grasp** | 30 yd | 2 Runes | Minor damage. Applies Death's Claim. |
| **Consume Fear** | 15 yd | 3 Runes | Minor damage. Generates Runic Power based on the number of targets. Applies Death's Claim. |
| **Remorseless Strike** | Melee | 3 Runes | Heavy single-target damage with moderate splash damage. |

## Runic Power Spells

| Spell | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Impending Doom** | TBD | TBD | Exploits Death's Claim by applying the Impending Doom effect/DoT. |
| **Death's Presence** | 30 yd | TBD | Applies the same Impending Doom effect to all targets in range. Higher Runic Power cost than the single-target version. |
| **Dominate Mind** | Melee | TBD | The target reflects damage it deals back to itself. The threshold/scaling increases with Death's Claim. |
| **Betrayal** | TBD | TBD | Reduces the target's damage dealt, scaling with Death's Claim. Intended as a defensive/situational ability rather than a core rotational spell. |

## Core Relationship

**Apply Death's Claim → generate Runic Power → spend Runic Power to exploit Death's Claim.**

Death's Claim is the persistent enemy-side state; it is not consumed by default.
